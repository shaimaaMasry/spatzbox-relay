Vision and mission

Why SpatzBox exists

Vision

Every neighbourhood in Ulm and Neu-Ulm gets what it needs from the businesses around the corner.

Mission

We connect local restaurants and shops with their neighbours through fair partnerships, reliable couriers and a delivery time we keep.

Local first

Every partner is a business from Ulm or Neu-Ulm. When they grow, the city grows.

Keep the promise

The time in the app is a promise to the customer, and to the partner whose food we carry.

Fair for all three sides

Merchants pay only on completed orders, couriers choose their own shifts, customers pay a clear fee.

How it works

A marketplace with three sides

SpatzBox earns money only when an order is completed. Merchants join for free and can leave at any time.

Customers

Order in the app and get a delivery window.

Pay €1.99–3.99 delivery, or SpatzBox+ €6.99/month

Merchants

Receive, accept and prepare orders on a tablet.

Pay 22% (restaurants) or 15% (stores) commission

Courier partners

Accept offers in the courier app, pick up, deliver.

Earn €4.50 + €0.60/km, +€1.00 per bundled order

Case brief · November 2026

SpatzBox keeps breaking its delivery promise

In one year, on-time delivery fell from 92% to 68%, while the company's target is 90%. Customers are leaving and the biggest restaurant partner is talking to a competitor. Your job: find out where the delivery process really breaks, and present a plan to the Supervisory Board on Thursday 3 December.
